# Idle Button — ButtonCorp™

An incremental / idle game that begins as a lighthearted corporate button-pressing job at **ButtonCorp™** and gradually uncovers a darker reality beneath the office.

## Gameplay & Features

- **Operations (AdVenture Capitalist / Communist style)**:
  - Progressively unlock 6 Button Divisions (*Coffee-Fueled Intern* up to *The Conduit*).
  - Cycle progress bars with manual `RUN` triggers or full automation via hireable **Managers**.
  - `x1` / `x10` / `MAX` bulk-buying and `2x` milestone multipliers at 10, 25, 50, 100, and 200 owned.
- **Dedicated Upgrades Panel**:
  - Separate **Upgrades** tab with **Managers & Automation** and **Equipment & Synergy** upgrades, plus an affordability badge.
- **R&D Lab & Active Protocols (Magic Research style)**:
  - Divisions generate **Research Points (RP)** on each cycle.
  - Complete timed Research Projects to unlock cooldown-based **Active Protocols** (*Synergy Rush*, *Power Surge*, *Deep Syphon*) and story revelations.
- **Restructure (Prestige)**:
  - Reset your run to claim **Executive Shares** for permanent global multipliers while the facility remembers your progress.

## Running Locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8080`.
